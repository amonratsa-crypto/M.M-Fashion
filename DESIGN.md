## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Audience
The brand communicates an understated Parisian-inspired chic fused with everyday Southeast Asian practicality. The audience includes university students, fresh graduates, and young working professionals seeking effortless wardrobe staples that bridge timeless capsule tailoring with seasonal micro-trends. The emotional tone is warm, confident, accessible, and poised—steering clear of cold, aloof haute-couture while retaining polished editorial refinement.

### Visual Style
The visual execution embodies **Warm Minimalism** paired with delicate tactile details:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Soft linen and warm alabaster canvases replace sterile high-key whites, infusing editorial warmth into e-commerce grids.
- **Editorial Polish:** High-contrast display serifs handle primary merchandising headlines, while neutral, humanized sans-serif type ensures effortless micro-reading across mobile checkout funnels.
- **Micro-Delicacy:** Subtle taupe hair-lines, whispered borders, and quiet background color layering structure the interface rather than heavy drop shadows or abrupt boxes.
- **Social Commerce Integration:** Clean native touchpoints for TikTok Shop, Instagram Lookbooks, and LINE/Facebook Messenger styling consultations are built seamlessly into the customer journey.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette derives from unprocessed cotton, linen canvas, and natural earth pigments:
- **Primary (`#1A1A1A` - Deep Onyx):** Serves as high-contrast typographic ink, primary interactive buttons, and graphic strokes.
- **Secondary (`#B89F82` - Warm Sandstone):** Anchors secondary active states, badges, accent borders, and lifestyle micro-elements.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` - Golden Taupe):** Highlights limited-edition promotions, sale callouts, star ratings, and celebratory cart badges.
- **Neutral (`#F6F3EE` - Warm Ecru/Alabaster):** Forms the foundational background atmosphere. Supporting tints include `#FAF8F5` for elevated card containers and `#EFECE6` for dividing rules and muted inputs.

### Semantic & Functional Accents
- **Success / In-Stock:** `#4A6B53` (Muted Sage) provides accessible verification without clashing with the beige undertone.
- **Error / Urgent Alert:** `#9E3A3A` (Terracotta Crimson) delivers WCAG AA compliance against `#F6F3EE`.
- **Social Commerce Links:** Tinted neutral pill containers utilizing brand glyphs with muted secondary hovers rather than harsh native social hexes, keeping the visual presentation unified.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines & Display:** `Playfair Display` conveys editorial elegance, poise, and timeless craftsmanship. Used for campaign heroes, collection launches, and major section titling.
- **Interface & Body:** `Inter` handles product naming, sizing matrices, descriptive copy, cart details, and checkout inputs. Thai language fallbacks automatically render via system `Noto Sans Thai` with proportional line-height matching.
- **Editorial Taglines & Sub-labels:** Uppercase `Inter` styled with wide tracking (`0.25em`) reflects the boutique logo lockup format (`SIMPLE. TIMELESS. YOU.`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px max content container with 48px outer margins and 24px gutters. Ensures ample negative space around tall 3:4 product lookbook imagery.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid layout with 32px margins and 16px gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid 100% canvas with 16px margins and 12px gutters, optimized for vertical thumb scrolling and two-column compact product grids.

### Spacing Density
Spacing leverages intentional asymmetry: generous vertical padding (`space-xl` to 4rem) separates curated narrative lookbooks, while product meta blocks and transactional controls maintain compact, accessible padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) for effortless one-hand interaction.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Stacking
Rather than dramatic synthetic drop shadows, depth is achieved through gentle tonal shifts and delicate contours:
- **Base Canvas:** `#F6F3EE` creates a calm foundational plane.
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Surfaces such as cards, filter menus, and dialogs sit on `#FAF8F5` or pure white `#FFFFFF` layered with a whisper border: `1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.07)`.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers (Cart, Quick View):** Elevated using an ultra-soft, low-opacity warm shadow: `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(92, 77, 62, 0.12)` accompanied by a gentle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with `rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.75)`).

## Shapes

### Shape Language
A soft, architectural radius language (`roundedness: 1`) preserves tailoring precision:
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards):** `0.25rem` (4px) subtly softens structural bounds without losing tailoring structure.
- **Floating Panels & Modals:** `0.5rem` (8px) for larger dialog viewports.
- **Tags, Status Pills & Social Badges:** Full pill execution (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for discrete categorizations, discount badges, and social media handles, creating a clear contrast between content cards and actionable utility chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#1A1A1A` background, pure white text, 4px border-radius, uppercase `label-md` tracking. Hover state transitions softly to `#333333`.
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #1A1A1A`, `#1A1A1A` text. Hover shifts to `#F6F3EE` with `#B89F82` border tint.
- **Subtle / Text Button:** Pure text with an animated bottom accent hairline in `#B89F82`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** 3:4 aspect ratio portrait frame. Default background `#FAF8F5`.
- **Overlays:** Top-left discount/new pill badge; top-right wishlist heart toggle.
- **Meta Block:** Category in muted uppercase `label-sm`, title in `Inter` 14px regular, and price in bold 14px with optional strike-through comparative price in `#8C827A`.

### Promotional Banners & Lookbook Strips
- Warm Sandstone (`#B89F82`) or Ecru (`#FAF8F5`) container blocks with centered serif titles (`headline-lg`), delicate framing hairlines, and primary CTA buttons.

### Badges & Tags
- **New In / Best Seller:** `#1A1A1A` pill badge with `#F6F3EE` text.
- **Sale & Promotions:** `#B89F82` pill badge with pure white text.
- **Fabric Tags (e.g., "100% Linen", "Cotton Blend"):** `#EFECE6` neutral tag with `#1A1A1A` body text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** `#FAF8F5` background, `1px solid #DED8CE`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, transition to `#1A1A1A` border upon focus with no outline glow.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Custom rounded selectors in `#1A1A1A` with ivory checks. Size variant selectors use rectangular boxes (4px radius) that turn deep black on selection.

### Social Commerce Links
- Pill-shaped action cards linking TikTok Shop, Instagram Lookbook, and Facebook Messenger. Styled with `#FAF8F5` surface, subtle `#DED8CE` outline, branded icon, and handle displayed in `label-md`.
